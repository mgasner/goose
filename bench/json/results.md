# JSON benchmarks

Measured 2026-10-16 on Darwin arm64, clang 21.0.0; load average 2.2 when the run started. Best of 50 runs each. Generated by `bench/json/json_bench.py`; the design is `docs/design/json.md`.

## Throughput (MB/s; write is per byte of output)

| File | Size | Parse | yyjson | Ratio | Parse + walk | yyjson | Ratio | Write | yyjson | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| twitter.json | 0.63 MB | 2653 | 5307 | 50% | 2347 | 4386 | 54% | 4446 | 7531 | 59% |
| citm_catalog.json | 1.73 MB | 4417 | 5682 | 78% | 3706 | 4838 | 77% | 2875 | 5498 | 52% |
| canada.json | 2.25 MB | 1561 | 2276 | 69% | 1263 | 1857 | 68% | 1105 | 1930 | 57% |

## The cursor (MB/s)

`index` is stage 1 alone. `find` is a cursor, then one lookup near the end of the text; `find (document)` is a whole parse, then the same lookup; `find (reader)` is a reader making the same lookup and then `finish`, so that the whole text is checked.

| File | Lookup | Index | Find | Find (document) | Find (reader) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| twitter.json | `search_metadata.count` | 2782 | 2077 | 2631 | 2806 |
| citm_catalog.json | `venueNames.PLEYEL_PLEYEL` | 3252 | 2558 | 4497 | 4879 |
| canada.json | `type.x` | 2827 | 2532 | 1561 | 2125 |
